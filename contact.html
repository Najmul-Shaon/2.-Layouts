Quick Answers

Common Questions

Do you offer home visits? +
Yes! We offer free home visits across all of Tamil Nadu. We come to you with all the necessary equipment to ensure a comfortable and convenient experience, especially for families with newborns.
Are the materials safe for babies? +
Absolutely. All materials we use are 100% non-toxic, baby-safe, and tested for use with newborns. We are a government-certified studio that adheres to the highest safety standards.
How long does the process take? +
The impression-taking itself takes just 2–5 minutes. The full casting and finishing process takes 7–14 days, after which we deliver the finished product to your door.
Do you deliver outside Tamil Nadu? +
Currently, our home visit service is available across Tamil Nadu. For other states, we can arrange a studio visit or discuss options — please contact us directly to find the best solution for you.
