About This Casting

Our Baby Hand & Feet Gold Frame is one of our most beloved keepsakes. Every casting is hand-crafted by our certified artists, capturing the tiniest and most precious details of your baby's hands and feet in stunning gold metallic finish.

Each piece comes mounted in a premium wooden frame with a personalized name plate and is ready to hang or display proudly in your home.

Is the impression material safe for my newborn? +
Yes, absolutely. We use certified alginate impression material that is 100% non-toxic, food-grade, and completely safe for newborns from Day 1. It's the same material used by dentists — completely harmless and gentle on baby skin.
How long does the impression process take? +
The actual impression takes just 2–5 minutes. The home visit including setup and cleanup is about 30–45 minutes total. We work around your baby's schedule for maximum comfort.
When will I receive the finished casting? +
After the impression session, your casting is completed within 10–14 working days. We'll update you at each stage and deliver directly to your door.
Can I choose a different frame or finish? +
Absolutely! All our castings are fully customizable. Choose from Gold, Silver, Bronze, or Natural White finishes, and Wooden, Glass, or Acrylic frames. We can also add custom engravings with name, date, and special messages.
