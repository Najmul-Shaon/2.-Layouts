Who We Are

Crafting Memories Since 2018

Freeze Ur Memories is a government-certified 3D casting and impression studio based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Founded in 2018 with a passion to help families preserve their most precious moments, we've grown into the region's most trusted name in memory casting.

With 7+ years of experience, we specialize in baby hand and feet impressions, couple casting, sibling casting, family 3D memories, and much more — all using 100% safe, baby-friendly materials tested and approved for use with newborns.

We offer free home service all over Tamil Nadu, ensuring comfort and convenience for our clients. All our creations are fully customizable and come with a lifetime guarantee because we believe your memories deserve to last forever.
