Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our 3D casting process, safety, pricing, and delivery.

🔬 Process & Experience

How does the 3D casting process work? +
Our certified artist visits your home with all equipment. We prepare a food-grade alginate impression material, gently immerse your baby's hand or foot (or both), wait 2–3 minutes for the impression to set, then carefully remove it. Back at our studio, we pour premium resin into the mould, apply your chosen metallic finish, and mount it in your chosen frame. The finished piece is delivered to your door within 10–14 days.
How long does the home visit take? +
The entire home visit typically takes 30–45 minutes, including setup, the impression-taking (just 2–5 minutes), and cleanup. We work around your baby's schedule to ensure everyone is comfortable and relaxed.
Can I choose the finish and frame style? +
Absolutely! All our castings are fully customisable. Choose from Gold Metallic, Silver Metallic, Bronze, or Natural White finishes. Frames are available in Solid Wood, Glass Base, or Acrylic Stand. You can also add personalised engravings with names, dates, and messages.
What if my baby moves during the impression? +
Our artists are experienced in working with babies of all ages, including very active ones! If a baby moves and the impression isn't perfect, we'll simply redo it at no extra cost. We always ensure the final impression is flawless before we leave.

🌿 Safety

Are the materials safe for newborns? +
Yes, 100%. We use certified, food-grade alginate — the same material used in dental impressions — which is completely non-toxic, hypoallergenic, and gentle on the most sensitive newborn skin. It is safe from Day 1 of birth. We are also a government-certified studio and follow strict safety protocols.
Is there any risk of allergic reaction? +
Alginate is hypoallergenic and reactions are extremely rare. However, if your baby has any known skin conditions or allergies, please let us know before booking and we will advise accordingly. Our artists are trained to handle any situation with care.

💰 Pricing & Booking

How much does a casting cost? +
Our prices start at ₹2,499 for a basic pet casting and range up to ₹7,499+ for premium couple or wedding sets. Baby castings start at ₹2,999. Family castings from ₹4,999. All prices include the free home visit and delivery. Visit our Services page for full pricing details.
Is the home visit really free? +
Yes! The home visit is completely free anywhere in Tamil Nadu. There are no hidden charges — the price you see includes the artist's visit, the impression-taking, the casting itself, the frame, and delivery to your door.
Do you offer any discounts or coupon codes? +
Yes! We offer seasonal discounts and special offers. Follow us on Instagram and Facebook to stay updated. You can also use coupon codes at checkout — for example, try FREEZE10 for 10% off your first order.

📦 Delivery & Guarantee

How long does delivery take? +
After the impression session, your casting is completed and delivered within 10–14 working days. We'll keep you updated at every stage and notify you when it's on the way.
What is the lifetime guarantee? +
Every casting from Freeze Ur Memories comes with a lifetime guarantee against manufacturing defects. If your casting is ever damaged due to a material defect (not accidental damage), we will repair or replace it at no cost to you.
Do you deliver outside Tamil Nadu? +
Our free home visit service currently covers all of Tamil Nadu. For customers in other states, we offer studio visits in Coimbatore. Shipping of the finished casting is available pan-India. Contact us to discuss options.
